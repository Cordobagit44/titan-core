# Ubiquitous Language

## Investigation
A persistent unit of work created to reduce uncertainty about a thesis.

## Thesis
The central claim an investigation seeks to support, refine, or refute.

## Hypothesis
An explicit assumption whose condition affects the thesis.

## Evidence
A source-grounded fact or observation that remains separate from its interpretation.

## Claim
An atomic statement extracted from or supported by evidence.

## Interpretation
A reasoned relationship between claims and hypotheses.

## Assessment
The current analytical state of an investigation. It is not a human decision.

## Decision
An action chosen by a human or an external system after considering an assessment.
